Our Story

A Texas Ranch Rooted in Tradition

Nestled in the piney woods of Deep East Texas, Chivoland is a family-operated ranch dedicated to raising quality goats, sheep, and cattle. We take pride in our animals and our land, maintaining the values of honest ranching that have guided us from the beginning.

Whether you're looking for breeding stock, meat animals, or simply want to see what we have, we welcome you to reach out. We raise our animals with care — on pasture, with good feed, and plenty of East Texas space to roam.
